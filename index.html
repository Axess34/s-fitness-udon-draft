About us · เกี่ยวกับเรา

Your gym close to home.

S Fitness is a private-feeling neighbourhood gym in Udon Thani: spacious, bright, never overcrowded, and kept spotless. Drop in for a day, join by the month, or train with one of our coaches.

Our members range from school and university students to athletes and older members. Age is no barrier. If you want to look after yourself, we'll help you get started and keep going.

  • Strength machines, free weights & racks
  • Treadmills & cardio zone
  • Air-conditioning, lockers, showers & clean restrooms
  • Coffee & snacks after your workout
